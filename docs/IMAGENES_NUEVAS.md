## Prompt de la cruz ankh

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: square catalog illustration for the Spanish website Tarot y Otras Artes, in the established Ritual nocturno style. Create an elegant photorealistic still life of a single small brass Egyptian ankh cross used as a symbolic object for a complementary relaxation session. The ankh has the correct oval loop at top, horizontal arms and long stem. Center the entire object on dark plum purple velvet draped over a dark wooden table, restrained dried lavender along one edge. Match a premium previous catalog photo of dark engraved rune stones on dark wood with a plum velvet pouch. Warm amber side lighting, realistic patina, tactile velvet, deep shadows, muted gold #E4C580 and purple #211B2F. Clear uncluttered composition, whole ankh visible with generous margins for responsive crop. No people, no text, no logos, no moons, no moon phases, no ouroboros. Opaque background.
